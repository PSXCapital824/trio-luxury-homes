WHERE WE

Our primary presence is in communities where luxury buyers value design, space, and long-term quality — within the most desirable corridors of North Atlanta.

Primary Markets

Johns Creek Primary Market

Large-lot estates and gated communities. Some of North Atlanta's highest price-per-square-foot luxury.

Alpharetta Primary Market

A premier destination for custom luxury. Walkable districts, top-tier schools, and strong long-term value.

Milton Primary Market

Equestrian estates and expansive acreage. The most exclusive residential land in the North Atlanta corridor.

Award-winning city planning, exceptional schools, and a growing base of discerning luxury buyers.

Cumming Primary Market

Rapidly appreciating market with lake access, open land, and strong demand for custom construction.

East Cobb Primary Market

Established prestige, mature neighborhoods, and consistent demand from move-up luxury buyers.

Brookhaven Primary Market

Intown luxury living with proximity to Atlanta's business core and a sophisticated buyer profile.
