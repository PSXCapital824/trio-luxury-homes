Standard With Every Trio Home

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Every plan ships with these finishes and features as standard — no negotiating, no surprises.

10 ft Ceilings — Main & Upper Level

10 ft ceilings on both floors as standard — a clear step above production-builder height.

Spray Foam Insulation — Attic

Closed-cell spray foam in the attic for superior thermal performance and reduced energy costs.

Tankless Water Heater

On-demand hot water throughout — no tank, no wait, lower energy use.

Architectural Shingles

Dimensional architectural shingles standard — longer lifespan and better curb appeal than 3-tab.

Hardwood & Tile Throughout

Engineered hardwood and tile across all living areas — no carpet standard.

Quartz Countertops & Tile Backsplash

Quartz countertops in the kitchen paired with a full tile backsplash.

Slim Shaker Cabinets — Kitchen, Baths & Laundry

Slim shaker profile throughout. Upgrade to modern flat-front frameless available — see upgrades below.

Frameless Glass Shower Doors — All Baths

No tub/shower combos. Every bathroom includes frameless glass shower enclosures.

Semi-Solid Core Interior Doors

Semi-solid core doors throughout — better sound dampening and a more substantial feel than hollow-core standard.

Soft-Close Hinges & Drawers Throughout

Every cabinet door and drawer in the home is fitted with soft-close hardware as standard.

LED Recessed Lighting Throughout

Full LED recessed lighting package across all rooms — energy efficient and professionally placed.

Black-Frame Windows & Exterior Doors

Large-format tempered glass windows with black aluminum frames inside and out — standard across all plans. Matched by high-end black exterior doors for a cohesive, architectural look.

Exterior — Painted Brick Front, Premium Siding

Painted brick on the front elevation. Remaining exterior finished in high-quality siding.

Smart Garage Door Openers & EV Outlet

Wi-Fi enabled openers with built-in camera on every bay, plus a dedicated EV charging outlet in the garage — both standard.

Tiled Front Patio

Front entry patio finished in tile as standard.

Sod & Driveway — Included

15 pallets of sod and a 600 sq ft concrete driveway pour included with every plan.

2 Electric Fireplaces Included

One electric fireplace in the living room and one in the loft area — both standard.

2-10 Home Warranty — Builder & 3rd Party

First year covered by Trio. Years 2–10 backed by a third-party 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty for structural and systems coverage.

Termite Treatment — First Year Included

Full termite treatment covered for the first year by Trio. After closing, buyers may elect to continue coverage through our preferred provider.
