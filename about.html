Who We Are

BUILT ON SOMETHING MORE THAN CONCRETE

Trio Luxury Homes was built on a straightforward idea: that a custom home should be a collaboration — one built on honesty, skill, and a genuine investment in the outcome. Not just for us. For you.

We're a boutique builder. Fewer projects means more attention, more accountability, and a better home at the end of every build. We don't take on every project — just the ones we can do exceptionally well.

Rooted in North Atlanta. We know these communities, these lots, and these markets. We're proud to build homes here that stand long after the signs come down.
