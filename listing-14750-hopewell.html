Architecture & Arrival

A Layered, Transitional Composition

The architecture of 14750 Hopewell Road is defined by a layered, European-influenced transitional composition. Steep gables, masonry at the lower level, smooth upper wall surfaces, and deeply articulated window groupings create depth and permanence across the façade — while large contemporary openings keep the exterior from feeling overly traditional.

Site & Outdoor Living

A 1.42-Acre Milton Estate Setting

At 1.42 acres, the property is large enough to create meaningful separation between the home, outdoor entertaining areas, and surrounding landscape while remaining manageable as a luxury residential estate. The site plan positions the residence within existing tree coverage, with a curving arrival sequence and a rear-yard relationship between the covered living areas and the planned pool/spa setting.

Covered outdoor living and dining sit directly off the interior gathering spaces, with an outdoor kitchen positioned for everyday use and larger events. The planned pool and spa extend the visual axis of the home into the landscape, and a dedicated pool bath allows the outdoor zone to function independently from the private interior rooms.

Interior

Signature Features

Main-Level Primary Suite

Conceived as a private wing rather than a single room: bedroom, expansive bath with freestanding tub and oversized shower, dual vanities, two water closets, separate dressing room, walk-in closet, morning bar, and secure storage.

Great Hall & Great Room

A central great hall connects the entry, formal dining room, and great room along one organizing axis, giving the main level openness and strong sightlines to the rear outdoor living space.

Kitchen & Scullery

A large island kitchen stays presentation-ready while a separate scullery and walk-in pantry handle prep, storage, and cleanup behind the scenes — built for both daily life and entertaining.

Main-Level Guest Suite

A second main-level bedroom suite provides valuable flexibility for guests or extended family without requiring stairs.

Junior Primary & Upper Lounge

Upstairs, a substantial junior primary suite with its own bath and dressing room is joined by three additional bedrooms and a dedicated lounge with wet bar — giving the upper floor its own identity.

Basement Family Room & Gym

The lower level adds a relaxed counterpart to the home's formal spaces: a family room with wet bar and gas fireplace, plus a dedicated home gym and bath accommodations.

Covered Outdoor Living & Planned Pool

Covered outdoor living, dining, and an outdoor kitchen connect the main interior spaces to a planned pool and spa environment — positioned as an extension of the home rather than a backyard add-on.

Formal Dining & Private Office

Dedicated rooms toward the front of the home — rather than spaces carved from one open-plan area — support both formal entertaining and everyday work-from-home needs.

4-Car Garage & Mudroom

A four-car garage connects to the main living areas through a tiled mudroom, keeping everyday household circulation organized and out of sight.

Location

Milton, Georgia

Milton offers a distinctive North Atlanta setting where estate-scale residential living, natural spaces, and a strong rural identity remain central to the community. The City of Milton describes itself as "rural but not remote," with a small-town feel, agricultural zoning, parks, greenspace, and long-standing equestrian heritage. For 14750 Hopewell Road, that setting is an important part of the property story: a 1.42-acre luxury residence that feels connected to the character of Milton while remaining within the broader North Atlanta market.
