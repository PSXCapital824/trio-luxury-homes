On 2.038 private acres along one of Milton's most coveted corridors, 15152 Hopewell Road represents the pinnacle of what Trio Luxury Homes builds. Outdoor Living

The Rear Grounds

Three distinct outdoor environments cascade from the rear of the home, each designed with its own purpose, character, and level of privacy. From an intimate enclosed courtyard to a resort-quality pool terrace to a wide private backyard below — this is outdoor living at a level rarely offered in a single home.

Space 01 The Courtyard

An intimate walled courtyard directly accessible from the main level creates a private transition from interior to exterior. Enclosed stone or masonry walls establish a sense of shelter and exclusivity — ideal for morning coffee, evening gatherings, or simply a moment apart from the larger grounds.

  • Direct main-level access from great room or kitchen
  • Walled enclosure for privacy and microclimate comfort
  • Outdoor kitchen or fireplace potential
  • Landscaped hardscape and garden design
Space 02 The Pool Terrace

The centerpiece of the rear grounds. A resort-caliber pool terrace spreads across the primary outdoor level, designed for entertaining at full scale. Wide stone decking, lounge areas, and pool-adjacent covered living spaces blur the boundary between inside and out — a setting that commands both attention and relaxation.

  • Gunite pool with custom geometry
  • Expansive stone decking and seating terraces
  • Covered outdoor living with fireplace option
  • Pool house / cabana-ready layout
  • Landscape lighting throughout
Space 03 Lower Level Backyard

Accessible directly from the walk-out basement level, the lower backyard offers a generous flat expanse that opens the property to its full 2.038-acre scale. Shaded, private, and removed from the activity of the terrace above — ideal for lawn games, recreation, a future sport court, or simply experiencing the depth and scale of the land.

  • Walk-out basement level access
  • Expansive graded lawn area
  • Wooded treeline border for natural privacy
  • Sport court or recreation area potential

Interior

Signature Features

Owner's Suite

Main-level owner's retreat with private sitting room, his-and-hers walk-in closets with custom built-ins, and a spa bathroom featuring dual vanities, soaking tub, and an oversized steam shower. Direct access to the courtyard.

Grand Entry & Foyer

Double-height entry foyer with sweeping staircase, stone flooring, and dramatic ceiling detail — the first statement in an uncompromising sequence of architectural moments. Formal dining and study flank the entry axis.

Chef's Kitchen & Scullery

Professional-grade kitchen with oversized island, Sub-Zero refrigeration, Wolf range, dual ovens, and an adjoining full scullery kitchen for entertaining. Custom cabinetry extends to the ceiling with integrated appliances throughout.

Great Room

Voluminous great room with coffered ceiling, stone fireplace surround, and a full wall of sliding glass that erases the boundary between interior and the courtyard beyond. The natural heart of the main level.

Bedroom Suites

All six bedrooms feature private en-suite baths with premium stone and tile selections. A dedicated main-level guest suite with separate entry, three upper-level bedroom suites, and a junior primary upstairs ensure flexibility for every household configuration.

Home Theater & Basement

The finished basement delivers a full entertainment level: dedicated home theater, wet bar and beverage center, recreation room, gym or flex space, and a full guest suite with private bath. Walk-out access leads directly to the lower backyard.

Study & Private Office

A richly appointed main-level study with custom millwork, built-in shelving, and coffered ceiling. Designed for a principal who works at the scale they live — private, elevated, and removed from the activity of the home.

Upper Loft & Lounge

A generous upper-level loft bridges the bedroom suites and serves as a secondary living hub — ideal as a media room, homework hub, or reading lounge. Overlooks the great room below, maintaining the visual grandeur of the double-height main space.

4-Car Garage

An oversized four-car garage with side-entry and tandem configurations, premium flooring, built-in cabinetry, and pre-wired EV charging stations. A separate service entrance and direct interior access via a well-appointed mudroom.
