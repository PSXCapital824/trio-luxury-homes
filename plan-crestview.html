The Crestview

Farmhouse character. Modern proportion. Built to last.
3,696 Sq. Ft.
4 Bedrooms
4.5 Bathrooms
3-Car Garage

The Crestview delivers the warmth and texture of Farmhouse architecture at a scale that never feels modest. A wide-open main level anchors the plan — great room, kitchen, dining, den, and a main-level junior master suite all on one floor — with three additional bedrooms, a bonus room, and dedicated laundry above. At 3,696 square feet across two stories, it's a plan that lives large without feeling oversized.

Plan Features
Great Room
23'4" × 16'10" — the heart of the main level. Open to kitchen and dining with direct access to the covered rear patio
Kitchen & Pantry
Open-concept with walk-in pantry, island seating, and a direct sightline to both the great room and dining area — designed for how families actually cook and gather
Dining
16'9" × 9'4" — open to the kitchen and positioned adjacent to the covered patio for seamless indoor-outdoor entertaining
Den
11'4" × 12'10" — private flex space off the entry foyer. Ideal as a home office, study, or formal sitting room
Jr. Master Suite
16'8" × 13'0" — main-level bedroom suite with private junior master bath and walk-in closet. Perfect for guests, in-laws, or single-level living
Master Suite
Upper-level primary retreat with a dedicated spa bath featuring soaking tub, dual vanities, and an oversized walk-in closet
Bedrooms 2 & 3
Two upper-level bedrooms each with their own walk-in closet — Bedroom 3 measures 14'4" × 17'1". Served by Bath 3 and Bath 4
Bonus Room
11'0" × 13'10" upper-level flex space — media room, playroom, home gym, or a fourth sleeping area for the right buyer
Covered Patio
14'0" × 11'0" primary covered patio off the great room plus a 9'0" × 9'4" sitting patio — two distinct rear outdoor zones on the main level
3-Car Garage
31'10" × 20'4" — oversized three-car garage with direct mudroom entry; EV-charging-ready
Laundry
Dedicated upper-level laundry room — positioned at the bedrooms, not the garage
Foundation
Crawlspace standard — slab available as an upgrade; demo of existing structure included if applicable
